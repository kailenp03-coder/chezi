Which areas do you cover?＋

Chezi Gardening Services is aimed at customers in Johannesburg and nearby suburbs. Send your suburb through to confirm availability.

Do you offer once-off and regular visits?＋

You can enquire about a one-time garden tidy-up or regular maintenance. The schedule will depend on availability and the work required.

How much does gardening cost?＋

Pricing depends on garden size, the type of work, how overgrown the garden is and whether green-waste removal is needed. Ask for a quote before booking.

Do I need to provide gardening tools?＋

Please mention what tools or equipment you have available when requesting a quote. Chezi can confirm what is needed for your specific job.

Can you take away garden waste?＋

Garden-waste removal can depend on the volume and disposal arrangements. Please ask when describing your job so it can be included in the quote if available.

How do I book a visit?＋

Use the contact section below to enquire. Include your suburb, the work you need and a convenient time for a response.
